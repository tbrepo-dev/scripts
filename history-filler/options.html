Resume

Load a PDF, Word (.docx) or text file. It fills in the sections below, so read them over afterward. The file is also kept here to upload to sites that ask for one.

Reading a resume sends it to Anthropic using your key. The key is also used by "Draft answers to open questions" in the popup, which sends your work history, skills, a cover letter if you saved one, and the job posting. Your email, phone and address are never sent. The key is stored unencrypted in this browser. Nothing else leaves your computer.

No API key? Use a Claude chat instead

Copy the instructions, paste them into a Claude chat with your resume attached, then paste the reply here. This doesn't keep a file for uploading, so add one with "Load resume" if you want that.

Cover letter

Optional. This same file goes to every application with a cover letter field, so use one that isn't written for a specific job, or skip this and upload your own each time.

Some applications have a text box for the letter instead of an upload. This is what goes in it, with each blank line starting a new paragraph. Loading a Word, text or PDF file above fills this in for you.

Job tracking sheet

Optional. When you click Submit on an application this extension filled, it adds a row to your Google Sheet with the position, company and today's date, and leaves Result blank for you. If the sheet has no Position / Company / Date Applied / Result header row, one is added at the top.

Set up the connector (once)

The extension can't write to Google Sheets by itself. This small script runs in your own Google account and adds the rows for it.

  1. Open your sheet and choose Extensions › Apps Script.
  2. Click Copy connector script above, replace everything in the editor with it, and click Save.
  3. Click Deploy › New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and pick Web app.
  4. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone, then click Deploy.
  5. Google asks you to allow access. Since it's your own script, it may say Google hasn't verified it: click Advanced, then Go to the project.
  6. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it above, and click Check sheet.

Anyone who has the connector link could add rows to your sheets, so keep it to yourself like a password. To use a different sheet later, just change the sheet link: the connector works with any sheet you can edit.

Skills and years

Add anything a job might ask about: software, tools, equipment, licences. A question that names it is answered: "Do you have experience with Excel?" gets Yes, and "How many years of Excel experience?" gets the number.

Your own answers

For written questions. When a question contains every word or phrase you list (for example "game credits"), the answer is typed in. Check it before you submit.

Common questions

Applications often ask these. Only answers you pick here are filled, and anything left blank is left alone. Read them before you submit, since a form may word a question differently. "Have you worked for this company" starts on No, but if the question names a company in your job history it's flagged for you instead.

Questions you've taught it

When an application asks something unusual, open the application, click the H icon, then "Teach a field", click the question, and choose "Always answer Yes" or "No". It's remembered by the question's wording, on every site, and listed here.

Self-identification

Optional. Forms ask these as voluntary questions. Only answers you pick here are filled, and nothing is guessed or taken from your resume. Anything left blank is left alone on the form. Like the rest of your profile, these are stored unencrypted in this browser.
